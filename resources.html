Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical construction timeline?

Most residential projects take 12-18 months depending on size and complexity. Our express delivery option can reduce this to 8-10 months with premium pricing.

How do I monitor progress if I'm an NRI?

We provide a dedicated app with daily photos, weekly video updates, quality reports, and 3D progress visualizations accessible from anywhere in the world.

What happens if construction exceeds the timeline?

Our no-delay policy ensures penalty clauses for us if we miss deadlines. You're protected through contractual guarantees and escrow arrangements.

Are there hidden costs after the initial estimate?

No. We provide transparent fixed-price contracts with detailed specifications. Any additional work is documented and approved before execution with clear pricing.

What warranty do you provide?

We offer a comprehensive 10-year structural warranty and 3-month post-handover maintenance period covering minor adjustments and punch-list items.
