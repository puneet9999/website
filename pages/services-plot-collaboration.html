What is Plot Collaboration?

Plot collaboration is a win-win arrangement where the plot owner and developer partner to build builder floors or plotted developments, sharing profits based on agreed terms.

You Own the Plot

You have a vacant or underutilized plot in a prime location in Faridabad or Gurgaon.

→

We Develop It

We handle approvals, design, construction, and sales — complete turnkey development.

→

You Profit

Receive pre-agreed share of profits or constructed units without any capital investment.

How Plot Collaboration Works

1

Site Evaluation

We assess your plot's location, size, potential FSI, and market value to determine profitability.

2

Project Planning

Detailed architectural design, feasibility study, and financial projections shared with you.

3

Agreement & Approvals

Legal agreement signed. We handle all statutory approvals and municipal clearances.

4

Construction

We execute the project with quality control, supervision, and regular progress updates.

5

Marketing & Sales

We market and sell the constructed units or deliver ready properties to you.

6

Profit Distribution

Post-sales, agreed profit share transferred to you per the collaboration agreement.

Typical Collaboration Models

70-30 Model

You receive: 70% of constructed area (built units)
We receive: 30% of constructed area (sold for profit)

Ideal if you want ready-to-use units at zero cost.

Profit Sharing Model

You receive: Pre-agreed percentage of net profit (e.g., 40-50%)
We handle: Complete development and sales

Ideal if you prefer cash returns over units.

Hybrid Model

You receive: Units + Cash returns
Customized arrangement based on plot size, location, and preferences.

Ideal for maximum flexibility and benefit.
