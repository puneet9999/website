Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical project timeline?

We guarantee 15-month completion for a standard home construction project. The timeline may vary based on project scope, size, and custom requirements.

Do you provide fixed-price contracts?

Yes. We provide transparent fixed-price contracts with detailed specifications, inclusions, and exclusions clearly mentioned. No hidden costs.

Can NRIs use your services?

Absolutely. We specialize in NRI construction services with 24/7 CCTV monitoring, weekly progress reports, and regular video updates.

Do you handle approvals and permits?

Yes. Our in-house team manages all statutory approvals, municipal clearances, and legal documentation.

What about post-handover support?

We provide a 3-month maintenance support period and are available for any issues after handover.

How do I get a free cost estimate?

Fill out our contact form or call us directly. We'll schedule a free consultation and provide a detailed estimate.
