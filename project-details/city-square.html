City Square

Status: Ongoing Expected: 2026 Location: Gurgaon

Project Overview

City Square is an ambitious mixed-use development transforming Gurgaon's commercial landscape. This mega-project spans 500,000 square feet and seamlessly integrates retail, office, and residential spaces to create a vibrant urban ecosystem. Designed for modern living and business, City Square will become a landmark destination.

The project features contemporary architecture, world-class infrastructure, and sustainable design principles. With excellent location and comprehensive amenities, City Square offers unmatched opportunities for businesses and residents alike.

Key Features

  • Retail Spaces: Modern retail units with high foot traffic and excellent visibility
  • Office Spaces: Premium office suites with flexible layouts and modern facilities
  • Residential Units: Luxury apartments for premium urban living
  • Food Court: Diverse dining options with international and local cuisines
  • Entertainment Zone: Cinema, gaming, and entertainment facilities
  • Connectivity: Perfect location with easy access to metro and major roads

Construction Quality

  • Structure: High-grade RCC with advanced seismic design standards
  • Flooring: Premium materials for retail, office, and residential spaces
  • HVAC Systems: Modern climate control for comfort and efficiency
  • Safety Systems: Advanced fire safety, emergency systems, and accessibility features
  • Technology Integration: IoT-enabled building management and automation

Sustainability & Green Features

City Square is built with environmental responsibility as a core principle. The development features solar energy generation, rainwater harvesting, waste segregation systems, and green building certifications. Sustainable materials and energy-efficient systems are integrated throughout to minimize environmental impact while providing a healthy and productive environment for businesses and residents.
