Urban Heights

Status: Completed Year: 2023 Location: Faridabad

Project Overview

Urban Heights stands as a pinnacle of luxury residential living in Faridabad. This premium apartment complex features 32 beautifully designed units spread across multiple floors with world-class amenities and modern infrastructure. Each apartment is crafted with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring comfort, functionality, and aesthetic excellence.

Located in a prime residential area with excellent connectivity to schools, hospitals, shopping centers, and business districts, Urban Heights offers the perfect balance of suburban tranquility and urban convenience.

Key Features

  • Premium Apartments: Spacious units with modern layouts and high ceilings
  • Full Amenities: Gym, swimming pool, community center, and recreation area
  • 24/7 Security: Professional security team with CCTV surveillance
  • Ample Parking: Covered and open parking spaces for residents
  • Green Spaces: Landscaped gardens and jogging tracks
  • Modern Architecture: Contemporary design with sustainable building practices

Construction Quality

  • Structure: Earthquake-resistant RCC frame with premium concrete grade
  • Flooring: Vitrified tiles and premium wooden finishes in bedrooms
  • Windows: Double-glazed windows with thermal and sound insulation
  • Electrical: Dedicated power supply with DG backup for 24x7 electricity
  • Plumbing: Advanced water management system with solar hot water

Sustainability & Green Features

Urban Heights is committed to environmental sustainability with features including rainwater harvesting, solar panel installations, waste management systems, and energy-efficient appliances throughout the project. The green spaces cover 40% of the total area, promoting eco-friendly urban living.
