Green Valley Homes

Status: Completed Year: 2022 Location: Faridabad

Project Overview

Green Valley Homes is a premier residential development located in the heart of Faridabad. The project comprises of 24 modern 3-bedroom apartments with contemporary architectural design and world-class amenities.

Key Features

  • Spacious Apartments: 3BHK units with 1,200-1,500 sq ft built-up area
  • Modern Amenities: Community center, gym, swimming pool, and children's play area
  • Landscaping: Lush green spaces and park view from every unit
  • Security: 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance
  • Parking: Dedicated parking spaces for each unit
  • Green Building: Energy-efficient design and rainwater harvesting system

Construction Quality

Every apartment in Green Valley Homes has been constructed using premium quality materials and the latest construction techniques. Our expert team ensured that every detail meets international standards of quality and durability.

  • Structure: RCC frame with 5-inch brick masonry walls
  • Flooring: Vitrified tiles in common areas, Italian marble in bedrooms
  • Windows: UPVC windows with weatherproof sealing
  • Electrical: ISI marked wiring with individual breaker system
  • Plumbing: PVC pipes with anti-bacterial treatment

Sustainability & Environment

Green Valley Homes was designed with environmental consciousness in mind. The development features several eco-friendly initiatives that reduce environmental impact and promote sustainable living.
