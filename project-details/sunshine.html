Sunshine Residency

Status: Completed Year: 2023 Location: Gurgaon

Project Overview

Sunshine Residency is a premium builder floor community located in the heart of Gurgaon's commercial district. This modern development features 18 meticulously designed floors with contemporary architecture and world-class amenities. Each unit is equipped with modern facilities including dedicated parking, high-speed lifts, and 24/7 security.

Spread across a prime location, the project offers excellent connectivity to major business centers, educational institutions, and shopping malls. The strategic placement ensures both convenience and accessibility for residents and businesses.

Key Features

  • Builder Floors: Premium residential floors with modern layouts and finishes
  • Parking Facility: Dedicated parking for each unit with secure access
  • High-Speed Lifts: Modern elevator systems for quick and safe access
  • Prime Location: Central Gurgaon location with excellent connectivity
  • Security: 24/7 professional security and CCTV surveillance
  • Modern Architecture: Contemporary design with aesthetic appeal

Construction Quality

  • Structure: RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete) frame with earthquake-resistant design
  • Flooring: Vitrified tiles and premium imported marble in common areas
  • Windows: Double-glazed windows with aluminum frame for sound insulation
  • Electrical: 100% backup power supply and individual meter connections
  • Plumbing: Hot water supply system and advanced drainage infrastructure

Sustainability & Green Features

Sunshine Residency incorporates sustainable building practices including rainwater harvesting systems, solar-powered common areas, and energy-efficient lighting solutions. The project has received recognition for its environmental initiatives and commitment to green construction standards.
